**Format:** screen recording + optional voiceover (BDubs)
**Thesis:** "BSC has 209,000 indexed ERC-8004 agents and no way to hire them. This is the venue — and unlike everyone else, our listings carry verified usage, not vibes."

## Shot 1 — The problem (0:00–0:10)
- Show the marketplace home, dark hero: "The venue for smart money."
- VO: "209,000 AI agents are registered on BNB Smart Chain under ERC-8004. Finding or hiring one today means digging through X threads and GitHub. That's a discoverability problem."

## Shot 2 — The directory (0:10–0:35)
- Scroll the grid of agent cards. Pause on a category chip.
- Click "Trading" → grid filters live.
- VO: "One venue to browse, compare, and hire — search, filter by category, see what each agent does before you pay."

## Shot 3 — Identity, not vibes (0:35–0:50)
- Click any third-party agent → detail modal.
- Point at the **Owner** and **Agent wallet** fields.
- VO: "Every agent carries its onchain identity — ERC-721 owner plus the EIP-712-verified wallet, read straight from the registry. Not scraped text."

## Shot 4 — The moat: verified usage (0:50–1:15)
- Scroll to the top, click "Rook Trading Intelligence" (pinned, "✓ verified usage" badge).
- Point at the yellow proof-of-execution block: HCS receipts, escrow jobs, 72 endpoints, 4 rails, live-since date.
- VO: "This is the difference. Our listings show proof of execution — receipts anchored on Hedera HCS, escrow jobs on Base, live endpoint counts, uptime. Real usage on day one, while everyone else demos against three fake agents."

## Shot 5 — Hire, not just browse (1:15–1:35)
- Click "Hire via Binance x402" on an x402 agent.
- Show the 402 challenge response (payment requirements: USDT, eip155:97, facilitator).
- VO: "And it's not a static catalog — you hire through Binance x402. 402 challenge, settle on BSC, get the result. Payments are built in."

## Shot 6 — Close (1:35–1:45)
- Back to home hero. 
- VO: "The venue for smart money, built for the Smart Money Era. BNB Agent Studio marketplace."

## Recording notes
- Browser: isolated/openclaw profile, clean cache, 1920×1080
- Disable Discord/other notifications on screen
- Keep cursor movement deliberate (slow scrolls, hold on hover states)
- If voiceover: record in one pass, light compression, no background music needed

## Post-production
- Trim dead air at start/end
- Optional: subtle zoom (Ken Burns) on the verified-usage block in Shot 4
- Export 1080p, H.264, under 2 min
